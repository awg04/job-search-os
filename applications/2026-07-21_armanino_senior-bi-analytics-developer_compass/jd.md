## Role in one line
Sr. BI/Analytics Engineer who bridges back-end data engineering and front-end reporting — building the "middle layer" (semantic models on curated Fabric tables) during a company-wide reporting transformation.

## Responsibilities
**Hands-on Fabric & transformation**
- Technical bridge between analytics needs and warehouse implementation; design/implement data models in Microsoft Fabric with the Data Warehousing team
- Contribute directly to data model development (SQL / Python); build & maintain Fabric notebooks for transformations, enrichment, and business logic
- Validate data outputs, logic correctness, and alignment with business rules before models are surfaced for reporting
- Partner on refresh sequencing, dependencies, and data readiness

**Semantic modeling & Power BI enablement**
- Design/maintain Power BI semantic models on curated Fabric tables; best practices for schema design, reusability, clear metric definitions and transparency
- Support the shift from reporting to insights; enable self-service

**Stakeholder translation & collaboration**
- Analytics partner to stakeholders; translate business questions into actionable info
- Facilitate working sessions between stakeholders and technical teams

**Process, standards & change management**
- Follow/contribute to modeling patterns, naming conventions, governance standards
- Use Git for version control, peer review, controlled changes
- Test, validate, document changes before production

## Requirements
- 3–5+ years in analytics, BI, data engineering, or related
- Strong hands-on: **Microsoft Fabric** (Lakehouse/Warehouse, notebooks, pipelines); **Power BI** (semantic models, datasets, basic DAX); **SQL** (advanced querying, transformations, performance); **Python** for data transformation (ideally PySpark)
- Solid understanding: dimensional modeling; fact vs dimension design; slowly changing dimensions; governance-friendly model design
