## Role Overview

The Power BI Engineer provides technical expertise for Kentro's Endpoint Support and Operations Monitoring (ESOM) program, focusing on business intelligence solutions. Responsibilities span the full software development lifecycle — planning, analysis, testing, integration, documentation, and implementation — to deliver secure, accurate reporting outcomes supporting program performance and strategic decision-making.

## Key Responsibilities

- Design, develop, and maintain Power BI dashboards, reports, and datasets across Endpoint divisions
- Build interactive visualizations communicating KPIs, performance trends, operational metrics, and financial data
- Analyze complex data requirements and develop efficient data models aligned with scalability best practices
- Integrate Power BI with enterprise systems including SQL databases, SharePoint, ServiceNow, and Excel
- Perform data cleansing, aggregation, and transformation using Power Query
- Participate in SDLC activities including planning, requirements analysis, testing, integration, and documentation
- Collaborate with stakeholders to define reporting needs and translate business questions into analytical solutions
- Conduct requirements gathering, stakeholder interviews, and data discovery workshops
- Document data sources, transformation logic, and reporting standards for maintainability
- Maintain governance best practices including dataset management, workspace organization, and data security
- Troubleshoot data quality, performance, and modeling issues
- Optimize dashboards for usability and performance
- Deliver training and presentations promoting data-driven decision-making adoption

## Required Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Business, Management, Information Systems, Engineering, or related field (or 8+ years relevant experience as substitute)
- 8 years of experience in business intelligence, analytics, software development, or related technical fields
- Demonstrated experience analyzing and solving complex problems requiring deep expertise
- Hands-on experience with Power BI Desktop and Power BI Service
- Strong proficiency with: data modeling and star schema design; DAX and Power Query; SQL and relational databases
- Experience integrating Power BI with enterprise platforms (ServiceNow, SharePoint, Excel)
- Experience supporting SDLC phases including planning, analysis, testing, integration, and documentation
- Ability to translate large, complex datasets into executive-level insights
- Experience supporting federal government programs or large, complex enterprise environments
- Strong communication, documentation, and stakeholder engagement capabilities

## Preferred Qualifications

- Relevant professional certifications (PMP, Power BI certification, data analytics credentials)
