Live Dashboard & Desktop Widget

Track your entire job search pipeline in one place. Real-time updates, smart automation, and desktop integration.

Live Pipeline Dashboard

Real-time KPIs: active roles, in-interview stage count, offers, and action items. Cards grouped by stage (Screen → Applied → Closed) with remote/comp/referral metadata and one-click deep links.

Recruiter Inbox Manager

Consolidated Gmail scan with fit scores, recommended actions, and draft replies. Hot recruiting opportunities highlighted. Log new contacts directly into your network tracker.

Job Prospects Tracker

Top-scoring roles from your daily briefing in one view. Filter by company, fit score, stage, and comp range. Posting links, email contact info, and LinkedIn deep links built in.

Desktop Widget

Zebar-powered desktop widget (~344×900) with dual tabs: Recruiters & Jobs. Auto-syncs every 15 minutes. Quick refresh button, ✨ full scan button, and persistent state across restarts.

Smart Auto-Sync

Dashboard state persists across browsers and devices. Archive/skip/run-status updates sync to disk automatically. No manual exports. One-click widget refresh pulls latest from all trackers.

Scheduled Refresh

Weekday mornings (~7:45 AM), your dashboard auto-refreshes from the latest briefing or runs a quick re-scan of top target companies. Fresh data waiting when you wake up.

Data Integrity by Design

Your dashboard reads from real tracker files (app-tracker.md, connection-tracker.md), not a database. Every card is a direct reflection of your work. Archive a role, change a stage, or run an action — it updates everywhere, immediately. No sync delays. No hidden state.
